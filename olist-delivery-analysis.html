Olist Delivery & Customer Experience

Investigating where late deliveries are concentrated and how they relate to customer reviews in historical Brazilian e-commerce data.

Project
Personal data analytics case study
Tools
SQL, MySQL, Python, data validation
Source
Olist dataset from Kaggle - Brazil, 2016-2018

The question

Which delivery problems deserve investigation, and how does customer feedback differ between late and on-time orders? I used SQL reporting queries and Python checks to connect operational data with a business question.

6.77%Late-delivery rate

Of 96,478 delivered orders, eight lacked required delivery dates and were excluded from delivery-rate calculations. An order is late when its actual delivery calendar date is after its estimated delivery date.

Late deliveries were associated with poorer reviews

Among reviewed eligible orders, low scores (1-2 out of 5) occurred in 62.40% of late orders and 9.27% of on-time orders. That is a 53.14 percentage-point gap, calculated before rounding.

This supports investigating both workload and relative performance, rather than ranking regions by rate alone.

How I approached the analysis

Define metrics before querying

Specified delivery eligibility, calendar-date lateness, review selection, and denominators. Kept missing outcomes separate from on-time deliveries.

Preserve one row per order

The SQL reporting design aggregates child tables before joining to an order-level fact table, avoiding inflated totals from multiple items, payments, or reviews.

Cross-check results

Recomputed delivery and review figures from the raw CSVs in Python. Reconciled regional counts and review coverage with saved SQL exports.

Separate findings from actions

Identified regions for further investigation without assigning seller fault or claiming that an intervention has already improved delivery outcomes.

Limits and next steps

This historical Brazilian dataset is not current Australian market evidence. Delivered-order analysis excludes undelivered outcomes; review comparisons cover reviewed orders only. Delivery labels describe whole orders and do not establish which seller caused a delay. A useful next step is to compare regions within purchase months and similar routes before testing operational changes.
